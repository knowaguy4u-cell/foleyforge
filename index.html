🧠 The learning engine

Every rating you give shifts your taste vector — the studio's model of what you like. New generations sample near your taste, so the forge literally evolves with you. This is continual learning: no model weights, no cloud, just your feedback loop.

How it works

01

Parse

A keyword engine maps your words to sound categories and synthesis parameters — no prompt leaves without a sonic interpretation.

02

Synthesize

Procedural Web Audio synthesis — filtered noise, FM, pitch envelopes, convolution-free reverb tails. Zero samples, zero API calls, infinite variation.

03

Learn

Your ratings re-weight the parameter space. Variants drift toward your taste over time — the studio that plays back your preferences.
