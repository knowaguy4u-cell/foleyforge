# FoleyForge — 2-minute demo + pitch script (~260 words)

## 0:00–0:15 — HOOK
"Every film, every game, every video lives or dies on sound. Studios pay thousands for sound-effect libraries — and they still sound like everyone else's. What if your sound studio lived in your browser… and learned your taste? This is FoleyForge."

## 0:15–0:50 — DEMO
"I type a scene — 'massive thunder cracking over a neon city in the rain' — and hit forge. The keyword engine parses it into layers: impact, city ambience, rain. Three variants synthesize live — procedural Web Audio, zero samples, zero API calls. Here's variant one… [PLAY]. Variant two… [PLAY]. Real waveforms, real audio, downloadable as WAV."

## 0:50–1:20 — THE LEARNING LOOP
"Here's the part that matters. I rate them — thumbs up, thumbs down. Watch the taste vector: my ratings just re-weighted the parameter space. Now I forge again… and the new variants have drifted toward my sound. This is continual learning — not a chatbot wrapper, the feedback loop IS the product. The studio that plays back your preferences."

## 1:20–1:45 — HOW IT WORKS
"Three stages. Parse: keywords map to synthesis parameters. Synthesize: filtered noise, FM, pitch envelopes, rendered through OfflineAudioContext. Learn: every rating moves an exponential moving average per category. One hundred percent client-side — no server, no cost, works offline."
